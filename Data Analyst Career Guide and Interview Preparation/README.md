**Completed:** June 2025 | **IBM Data Analyst Professional Certificate**

## 📊 Course Overview

This module bridges the gap between academic data analytics concepts and professional industry practice. It explores the landscape of the data analytics job market, standard business reporting requirements, and comprehensive preparation strategies for both technical SQL/Excel assessments and business case interviews. Through focused exercises, this course builds the core competencies needed to clean messy data under time constraints, translate raw numbers into actionable stakeholder insights, and present a polished analytics portfolio.

---

## 🎯 Key Findings

* **The Role of the Modern Analyst** - Industry analysis indicates that top-tier data analyst roles prioritize candidates who can go beyond data extraction to provide diagnostic and prescriptive insights that influence business decisions.
* **Core Technical Milestones** - Technical screening processes heavily favor speed and accuracy in writing relational queries (SQL), designing structured pivot tables, and mapping clean data pipelines.
* **Business-to-Tech Translation** - The most critical differentiator in behavioral and case interviews is the ability to communicate technical findings clearly to non-technical stakeholders using frameworks like the STAR method.

## 📊 Data Source

---

## 💡 Key Skills Demonstrated

* **Data Profiling & Aggregation** - Writing highly efficient SQL queries to calculate business-critical metrics such as month-over-month growth, customer churn, and regional revenue performance.
* **Spreadsheet Modeling** - Constructing dynamic, error-free spreadsheets to rapidly isolate data trends and summarize high-volume operational metrics.
* **Stakeholder Communication** - Presenting complex analytical findings through concise summaries, focusing heavily on the "so what?" behind the data.
* **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** - Systematically auditing new datasets to detect missing values, inconsistencies, and key structural patterns prior to reporting.

---

## 📉 Visualization Highlights

* A critical skill evaluated during data analyst interviews is the selection of the right chart to tell a clear business story. This module emphasizes foundational visual analytics:
  * **Metric Distribution & Variation** - Employing histograms and box plots to break down performance variability, evaluate range spreads, and isolate statistical outliers in business operations.
  * **Correlation & Trend Discovery** - Utilizing scatter plots to visually evaluate relationships between key metrics and implementing bubble charts to add third-dimension context (such as volume or cost) to executive summaries.
